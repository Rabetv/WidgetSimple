# WidgetSimple — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 23 October 2026_

WidgetSimple does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

- **No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.** The app contains no third-party SDKs.
- **Your widget settings** (chosen apps, fonts, colours, layout) and any fonts you import
  stay on your iPhone, in the app's own storage and its App Group shared with the widget.
  They are removed when you delete the app.
- **Purchases** are made through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we
  receive no payment details, name or e-mail address. The app only asks the App Store
  whether the WidgetSimple Pro subscription is active on your Apple Account.
- **Children.** The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.
